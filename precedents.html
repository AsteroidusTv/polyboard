Past market accuracy

Default horizons follow the scheduled market duration. D means days, H hours, M minutes and S seconds before official Polymarket resolution. Above 50%, Yes is favored. Below 50%, No is favored. A 50% price has no favorite. The signed gap is the final Yes value minus the historical Yes price. Each quote must come from a complete observation before the cutoff and be recent for that horizon. Average error and calibration require five markets across three events.

Price calibration

At the selected horizon, markets are grouped by their historical Yes price. Compare each group’s average Yes price with how often Yes actually won. Brier skill measures the relative change in squared forecast error versus always predicting 50%. Small samples can show large gaps by chance.
